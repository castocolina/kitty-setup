<objective>
Make an absent colour absent and make the passthrough admit only the one form kitty actually accepts
(D-12, as amended by U-2 from measurement).

Purpose: two anti-patterns meet in three small files. `normalize_color` returns `None` to mean "absent",
and two call sites rescue that `None` with a truthiness fallback that hands the raw input back — so an
empty string reaches the kitten and fails there instead of being handled here. And the passthrough rule
is "contains a colon or a parenthesis", which admits `foo:bar`, `rgba(...)`, `hsl(...)` and three other
forms kitty 0.49.0 rejects outright. Both produce the same user-visible failure: a half-built scene and
an error from kitty rather than from us.

The correction matters more than the extension. `tests/unit/test_scenes_colors.py:124` currently asserts
that `rgba(1,2,3,0.5)` passes through — it encodes the opposite of the requirement, and an executor who
only *adds* cases will leave it green while shipping the defect.

Output: `tmp/h32-color-forms/` recreated **first**, reproducing both defects before any edit; a RED
commit holding the inverted assertion, new unit cases for each measured-rejected form and each absence
case, and one real-kitty case, proven failing from its own tree; then an anchored `rgb:`-only
passthrough and absence branches in `background.py` and `accent.py`.
</objective>
