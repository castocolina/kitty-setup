**IN-03. Decision: remove the asides on a full uninstall, keep them whenever backups are kept, and name

- **Research.**
  - An aside is our previous payload, a copy of a `config/kitty` tree this tool shipped. Every install

    user ownership than the live payload a full uninstall already deletes.
  - A full uninstall also deletes every `kitty.conf.bak.*` (the user's own conf copies) after the
    prompt lists them.
  - `--keep-backups`, which `make clean` passes, and `--keep-config` exist to keep prior state.
  - A payload kept as a symlink into a dotfiles repo gives a symlinked aside. `shutil.rmtree` refuses
    it; `Path.unlink` removes the link and never follows it.
- **Experiment** (child process, HOME is a scratch dir):

    symlink).

    full uninstall, and names each.
  - It keeps and names all four otherwise.
  - It never touches `kitty-setup.rollback-mine`, `….notes`, `kitty-setup.rollbackX` or `…Z.1.bak`.
- **Why removal rather than listing as kept.** Four places promise a full uninstall removes
  everything: the Makefile help, `README.md`, the `cli_uninstall` docstring and `uninstall.sh`'s
